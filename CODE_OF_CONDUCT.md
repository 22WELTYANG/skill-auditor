# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want collaboration here to be safe, respectful, and evidence-driven.
Contributors should:

- discuss people and ideas with patience and professional respect;
- give actionable technical feedback and assume good faith;
- protect private target content, credentials, and vulnerability details;
- distinguish verified behavior from suspicion or speculation; and
- accept correction when evidence changes a conclusion.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate
misrepresentation, and publishing another person's secrets or private security
report are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other public spaces where someone represents this project. Maintainers may
edit or remove harmful content and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact methods
on the GitHub profile of
[`@22WELTYANG`](https://github.com/22WELTYANG). Do not use a public issue when a
report would expose private information. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and avoided.
